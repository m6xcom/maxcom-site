Method and limits
  • Snapshot at one block, refreshed hourly. Market totals, positions and oracle prices are read with eth_call at that block; borrower lists and vault allocations come from Morpho's public API.
  • Liquidatable debt applies a uniform drop to the oracle price of the collateral. The liquidation bonus is Morpho's LIF = min(1.15, 1 / (1 − 0.3 × (1 − LLTV))).
  • Exit cost uses the token's highest-volume USDG pools only (Uniswap v3 / v4 incl. fee-only hooks, Ramses V3, Slipstream). It assumes the pools look like they do now when the drop happens. "≤" marks an upper bound: some candidate pool ran past the price range loaded for it. ">71%" means every route did.
  • Feed age is the time since the oracle's stock feed last updated. Feeds do not update from Friday close to Sunday evening ET, so 48–57 h is normal on weekends. The custom oracle used by the largest markets reverts when its feed is older than 96 h; MorphoChainlinkOracleV2 has no staleness check.
  • Not investment advice. Point-in-time data; errors are possible.

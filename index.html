Research

Read-only measurements on Robinhood Chain; every number comes from on-chain data and can be reproduced.

Market structure · Oct 2026

Stock tokens trade when the US market is closed

16 stock tokens, 1,993 USDG pools, two full weeks of swaps: $651.6M of volume, 55% of it outside the regular 9:30–16:00 ET session.

The weekend DEX price follows the Monday open: correlation 0.97 and the right direction on 29 of 32 token-weekends, mostly by tracking futures and 24-hour venues from Sunday evening.

Lending risk · Nov 2026

Stock-token collateral on Morpho

23 stock-collateral markets, $1.93M supplied, $1.70M borrowed, mostly by a handful of large borrowers. Oracle feeds freeze from Friday close to Sunday evening (48–57 h).

DEX depth, not collateral, is the binding constraint: GOOGL pools absorb about $29k at 2% price impact, against about $308k of collateral that a 20% drop would make seizable. No large stock-collateral liquidation has happened on the chain yet; all-time liquidation bonus on stock markets is about $200.
